Leximax Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 4, 2026

Leximax is a vocabulary learning application that allows users to create their own dictionaries, organize words into packages, and practice them through training exercises.

Information We Collect

Leximax does not require users to create an account and does not collect personal information such as names, email addresses, phone numbers, location, contacts, photos, or other personal data.

The words, translations, packages, and other vocabulary data created by the user are stored locally on the user's device.

Data Transmission

Leximax does not operate its own servers and does not transmit users' vocabulary data to the developer or to third-party services.

Backups

Depending on the user's device, Android version, account settings, and backup settings, application data may be included in backups provided by the Android operating system or Google. These backups are managed by the operating system and the user's account settings, not by Leximax.

Third-Party Services

Leximax currently does not integrate advertising, analytics, crash reporting, or other third-party services that collect user data.

Data Deletion

Users can delete their words and packages from within the application. Leximax also provides an option to delete all application data stored by the app.

Uninstalling Leximax removes locally stored application data from the device, subject to any backups independently maintained by the operating system or the user's account.

Children's Privacy

Leximax does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Since the application does not require an account and does not collect personal information, no personal information is knowingly collected from children through the application.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if Leximax's functionality or data practices change. Any changes will be published on this page with an updated revision date.
